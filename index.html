01 About

A media plan is an argument

Every slot you buy is a claim about where attention actually is, and you have to defend it — before the campaign with an evaluation, after it with the numbers.

I spent two years making that argument at Omnicom Media Group in Mumbai, planning across print, radio and digital while building up TV, for FMCG, financial services and consumer-tech brands. Much of it was launch work, where there is no prior performance to lean on and the plan has to stand on research and judgement alone.

I'm now at Alliance Manchester Business School reading for an MSc in Marketing — turning practitioner instinct into something I can evidence properly, through customer insight, research methods and strategy. I'm looking for graduate roles in media planning, strategy or brand marketing in the UK.

Trained on BARC Yumi Analytics and TAM Media Research. Worked on launch campaigns for Marico, Shriram Finance and Atomberg, each straight off a won pitch.

05 Selected work

Wolves Records — taking a football club's label to India

University of Wolverhampton × Wolverhampton Wanderers FC · team of six ("Mind Hive") · 25-slide strategy · runner-up

The brief

Wolverhampton Wanderers had launched Wolves Records with Warner Music UK's ADA — a label with a football club's distribution behind it and no music audience of its own. Our brief was a market-entry strategy for India.

The answer

Sound War — a timed composition contest pairing emerging artists with producers, with a Wolves Records deal for the winning team. It solved both of the label's problems with one mechanic: an A&R pipeline that doubles as the launch campaign, and a reason for a football brand to be talked about by people who don't follow football.

Why India, and who for

Gen Z, 13–24. The audience read came from a market where people listen to roughly 19 hours of music a week against a global average of 18, and where the industry was compounding at around 15% a year — but where a club-owned Western label has no natural entry point.

What sat underneath it

Industry structure, STEEPLE, SWOT, segmentation and positioning, the full marketing mix, a 24-month rollout, a media kit, cost-benefit analysis and a CSR plan.
